Privacy & Confidentiality Policy

Last Revised: September 2026 • Document Version 1.4

Compliance Notice: This privacy policy constitutes the operational framework and data handling terms for Trust Funds Recovery. All policies are subject to ongoing refinement and official compliance verification.

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality

At Trust Funds Recovery, we recognize that individuals and businesses contacting our organization are often dealing with sensitive financial distress, unauthorized asset transfers, or complex legal disputes. We treat all personal, corporate, and financial disclosures with the highest tier of confidentiality under strict Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) principles.

2. Categories of Information Collected

In order to evaluate dispute viability and compile forensic evidence dossiers, we may collect:

  • Identification & Contact Data: Full legal name, email address, telephone number, country of residence, and government identification where required for statutory filings.
  • Financial Loss & Transaction Data: Bank statements, SWIFT MT103 confirmations, cryptocurrency transaction hashes (TxIDs), credit card dispute slips, and payment gateway receipts.
  • Correspondence Records: Emails, platform chat logs, contract agreements, terms of service, and recorded verbal exchanges with counterparties.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized browser identifiers, IP address timestamps for session security, and intake completion metrics.

3. How Information Is Utilized

Collected information is used strictly for legitimate casework purposes:

  • Conducting preliminary feasibility and viability audits.
  • Reconstructing transaction timelines and identifying counterparty routing accounts.
  • Drafting evidence dossiers for presentation to banks, ombudsmen, and law enforcement agencies.
  • Communicating case milestones and strategic recommendations to you.

We do NOT monetize, broker, or sell personal or financial data to third-party marketing firms under any circumstances.

4. Data Security Protocols & Encryption

All digital documentation submitted via our portals is encrypted using AES-256 standards at rest and transmitted using TLS 1.3 encryption protocols. Access within Trust Funds Recovery is strictly segregated via role-based authentication: only caseworkers and forensic analysts directly assigned to your case file are granted document inspection credentials.

5. Data Retention & Erasure

Client documentation is retained solely for the active duration of the case evaluation and any subsequent institutional dispute proceedings. Following formal case closure or upon written client request, all uploaded financial exhibits and identification records are permanently sanitized and purged in accordance with our data destruction protocols.

6. Sharing with Third Parties

Information is shared only under the following strictly defined conditions:

  • With your explicit, written authorization to formal dispute recipients (e.g. intermediary banks, regulatory ombudsmen, merchant acquiring processors).
  • Pursuant to valid legal process or court order issued by a competent court of applicable jurisdiction.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA regulations), you maintain the right to:

  • Request a copy of all personal records maintained in your case file.
  • Demand correction of any inaccurate information.
  • Request immediate deletion or cessation of processing (Right to be Forgotten).
  • Revoke intake consent at any time prior to formal institutional filing.
