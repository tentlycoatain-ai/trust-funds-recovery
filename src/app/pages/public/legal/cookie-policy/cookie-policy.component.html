1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are compact data files deposited onto your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They are universally employed to make web properties operate reliably, remember user state, and provide security authentication.

2. Categories of Cookies Employed

Trust Funds Recovery strictly classifies cookies into three operational tiers:

  • Session Integrity & Security (Strictly Necessary): These cookies enable secure HTTPS cryptographic connections, prevent cross-site request forgery attacks on intake forms, and verify that you are communicating directly with our authenticated servers.
  • Anonymized Analytical Metrics: These cookies collect non-identifiable telemetry (such as browser type, page response latency, and intake error rates) to help us ensure our site functions properly across worldwide connections.
  • Session Preferences: Temporary cookies that recall interface preferences (such as high-contrast display modes or draft form states) while you complete an intake submission.

3. Third-Party Cookies & Marketing Pixels

Given the sensitive nature of financial recovery casework, we strictly prohibit third-party behavioral advertising pixels (such as social media retargeting tags or third-party ad networks) from tracking your activity across our domain.

4. Managing Cookies Via Your Browser

In addition to the interactive preference toggle provided above, you can configure your browser (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, Microsoft Edge) to refuse or delete cookies. Please note that disabling strictly necessary security cookies may prevent encrypted intake forms from transmitting successfully.
