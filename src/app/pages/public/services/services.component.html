The Assessment Protocol

How Every Engagement Commences

To ensure we deliver high-impact assistance, all new matters follow a three-tier intake qualification protocol.

Phase 1

Secure Submission

You provide transaction receipts, communication records, and a concise summary through our encrypted intake portal.

Phase 2

Preliminary Audit

Our senior analysts review the transaction pathways, identify counterparty entities, and evaluate recovery viability.

Phase 3

Engagement Roadmap

If feasible, we furnish a tailored engagement proposal outlining specific deliverables, costs, and projected timelines.
