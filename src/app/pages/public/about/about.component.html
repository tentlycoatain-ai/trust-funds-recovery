About Our Organization

Integrity, Transparency, and Forensic Discipline

Who We Are

Bridging the Evidentiary Gap in Financial Disputes

When financial deception or unauthorized transactions occur, victims face immense hurdles: ambiguous international jurisdictions, automated bank rejection notices, and deep emotional distress.

Trust Funds Recovery operates as an objective, forensic advocacy partner. We step into the gap between aggrieved clients and institutional authorities by assembling rigorous, documented evidence packages that comply with legal and banking standards.

We operate with an uncompromising commitment to realism: we evaluate every case with clear-eyed objectivity, declining matters where recovery is factually impossible, and dedicating our full analytical resources where viable avenues exist.

Methodology

How We Approach Complex Financial Disputes

Informal complaints to banks or regulatory bodies are almost universally dismissed due to lack of standard documentation or unclear timelines. Our methodology transforms chaotic evidence into authoritative case files:

A
Transaction Chronology & Audit

We build a minute-by-minute timeline linking communications, platform promises, fund transfers, and subsequent defaults.

B
Jurisdictional & Entity Mapping

We unmask intermediary accounts, payment service processors, and host institutions governing the recipient entities.

C
Actionable Legal & Regulatory Packaging

Our completed dossiers are structured specifically for presentation to ombudsmen, compliance officers, and legal counsel.

Case File Composition

  • Executive Case Summary Clear chronological narrative of loss
  • Banking & Ledger Exhibits Verified wire slips & transaction hashes
  • Counterparty Assessment Registry search and regulatory warning checks
  • Statutory Dispute Notices Formal claim filings tailored to merchant & banking rules
