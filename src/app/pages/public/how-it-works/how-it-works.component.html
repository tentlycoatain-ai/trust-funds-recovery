Preparation Guide

What Documentation Will You Need?

Gathering your records early dramatically accelerates the initial feasibility audit. We recommend locating the following materials before completing your intake:

Important Tip: Do not delete chat logs or alter electronic documents. Even seemingly minor message timestamps can serve as critical proof of counterparty deception.
Banking & Payment Confirmations
  • SWIFT wire receipts (MT103 message with reference code)
  • Bank statements showing debited accounts and recipient details
  • Credit/debit card transaction statements
  • Payment gateway confirmation receipts (e.g. PayPal, Stripe)
Communications & Correspondence
  • Email threads with full internet headers if available
  • Exported messaging app histories (WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal)
  • Platform support tickets or live chat transcripts
  • Audio recordings or phone logs with dates and durations
Platform Records & Agreements
  • Account dashboard screenshots displaying balance & transactions
  • Signed terms of service, advisory contracts, or investment terms
  • Withdrawal rejection notices or fee demand messages
  • Public ledger transaction hashes (TxID) for digital assets
Setting Expectations

Realistic Dispute Timelines

Financial recovery is governed by institutional statutory review cycles, not instantaneous automation. Here is what to anticipate:

24–48 Hours
Intake & Feasibility Screening

Initial review of facts, confirmation of jurisdiction, and preliminary viability score.

5–10 Business Days
Evidence Assembly & Dossier Production

Forensic document indexing, transaction trail mapping, and drafting formal statutory notices.

30–90 Days
Institutional Review & Mediation Cycles

Statutory response window for recipient banks, card networks, and financial ombudsmen.
