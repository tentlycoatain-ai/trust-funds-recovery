Immediate Actions If You Suspect Financial Compromise

Time is the most critical variable in financial disputes. If you suspect an unauthorized transaction or fraudulent entity, take these four immediate steps:

1

Notify Sending Institution

Request an immediate wire recall or fraud freeze via phone and immediately follow up with a written confirmation letter.

2

Secure All Credentials

Reset passwords on all associated email and financial portals using a clean, secure device and enforce multi-factor authentication (MFA).

3

Download All Evidentiary Records

Export conversation logs, balance statements, transfer receipts, and platform terms before the counterparty restricts your access.

4

Initiate Case Feasibility Review

Submit your documentation to our intake unit to determine if viable institutional dispute avenues remain open.
